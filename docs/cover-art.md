공통 프롬프트: One continuous flat wraparound book jacket painting, landscape 3:2. Left half back cover, right half front cover, no seam. Original literary artwork, not recreating published covers. Simple loose oil/acrylic painting, few large shapes, rough broad strokes, matte texture. Motif lower right; upper right quiet negative space for typography; left sparse for prose. Full bleed. No text, mockup, book object, logos or panels.

- **demian**: self discovery: a small dark bird emerging from a cracked ivory egg, deep slate blue and muted gold, poetic
- **justice**: moral philosophy: a simplified balancing stone sculpture of two asymmetric red and ivory blocks balanced on a narrow charcoal support, quiet grey
- **economics-concert**: everyday economics: a tiny white coffee cup and a few abstract ochre coin circles, moss green and cream, simple still life
- **vegetarian**: quiet resistance and transformation: a single dark green plant stem emerging from an abstract human torso shape, muted pale sage and dusty rose, spare and sensitive
